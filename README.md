#  Dabarena – Your Premium Chess Arena

  <strong>A modern, mobile-first chess experience featuring bilingual support, mischievous AI, and immersive gameplay.</strong>

## 🎯 Key Features

### 🤖 Intelligent Opponents
- **Play vs Dabaru AI**: Powered by our very own Dabaru AI, offering 5 levels of difficulty from Beginner to Master.
- **Play vs LLM**: Experience the next generation of chess AI. Challenge an LLM-powered opponent that doesn't just play moves, but understands the game's flow and strategy in a human-like way.
- **Mischievous Personality**: The AI isn't just a machine. "Dabaru" is known for its witty, mischievous, and context-aware taunts in both English and Bengali, making every match feel alive.

### 👥 Multiplayer & Social
- **Local Multiplay**: Play with a friend on the same device with a perfectly optimized top-down view.
- **Bilingual Interface**: Seamlessly switch between **English** and **Bengali (বাংলা)**.

### 🎨 Personalization & Immersion
- **Custom Board Themes**: Choose from Classic Teal, Wooden Brown, or Ocean Blue to suit your style.
- **Unique Avatars**: Personalize your profile with a variety of handcrafted avatar options.
- **Immersive Sound System**: High-quality audio feedback for every move, capture, check, and victory.
- **Interactive Tutorials**: Learn the game or sharpen your skills with built-in rules and guides.

---

## 📱 Mobile-Native Experience

This app isn't just a wrapper; it's optimized for a premium Android experience:

- **Grandeur Splash Screen**: A beautiful, dark-themed entrance that sets the stage for a grand game of chess.
- **Adaptive UI**: The app automatically detects your system's light/dark mode and adjusts the status and navigation bars for a seamless look.
- **Edge-to-Edge Design**: Fully immersive gameplay that utilizes your entire screen, hiding distractions while keeping vital system info visible.
- **Performance Optimized**: Smooth transitions and high-performance WebView settings ensure a lag-free experience.
- **Smart Navigation**: Native back-button support for easy navigation through menus and game history.

---

## 🚀 How to Play
1. **Launch**: Open Dabarena and enjoy the smooth splash transition.
2. **Choose Your Challenge**: Select between the Stockfish-powered AI, the new LLM opponent, or a friend.
3. **Customize**: Set your preferred language, board theme, and timer.
4. **Dominate**: Move your pieces with a simple click-to-move interface and listen for the satisfying sound of a checkmate.
